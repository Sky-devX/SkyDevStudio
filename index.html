What is SkyDev Studio?

SkyDev Studio is the central Windows app where you can find SkyDev programs, news, and updates.

How do new programs appear in the app?

The SkyDev catalog gets updated. Once new programs are released, they appear in the Apps section.

Can I control updates myself?

Yes. In Settings, choose whether updates download automatically. You can track active downloads in the app.
